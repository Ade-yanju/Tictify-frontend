Tictify — Event Ticketing for Nigeria

Tictify is a Nigerian event ticketing platform where organisers sell tickets online and attendees pay securely and receive a unique QR-code ticket. Browse live events, buy tickets with card or bank transfer through Paystack, and get admitted in seconds at the gate.

For event attendees

Discover concerts, parties, festivals and shows across Nigeria on our events page. Pick your ticket type, pay online, and your QR-code ticket arrives by email — show it at the entrance for fast, secure entry. Lost your ticket? Recover it anytime from “Find my tickets”.

For event organisers

Create an event in minutes, set multiple ticket types and prices, and start selling. You keep the full ticket price — platform and payment fees are added on top and paid by the guest. Track sales in real time, scan guests in at the gate from any phone, and withdraw your earnings straight to your Nigerian bank account.

Why Tictify

  • Secure QR-code tickets with fast gate check-in
  • Card and bank-transfer payments via Paystack
  • Real-time sales dashboard and automatic payouts
  • Discount codes, early-bird pricing and group tickets
  • Promoter and affiliate links to grow your sales
